Allen Yuan

Office: Lunt B18

I am an assistant professor at Northwestern University. Previously, I was a member at the IAS ('23-'24) and a postdoc at Columbia University ('20-'23). I did my PhD at MIT, advised by Jacob Lurie, and my undergraduate work at Harvard.

I am interested in various aspects of homotopy theory, especially as they relate to algebraic K-theory, arithmetic geometry, and classical problems in algebraic topology. My CV can be found here.
